## 🟢 STAGE 1: Azure Fundamentals – Interview Questions

### 🔬 Labs Covered

* Azure Portal
* Resource Groups
* Subscriptions & Pricing
* Global Infrastructure

---

### ✅ Core Interview Questions

1. What is Azure and how is it different from on-prem infrastructure?
2. What is a **Resource Group** and why is it important?
3. Can a resource belong to multiple resource groups?
4. What happens if you delete a resource group?
5. What is the **shared responsibility model** in Azure?
6. What are **regions** and **availability zones**?
7. How does Azure pricing work at a high level?

---

### 🎯 Scenario Question (Very Important)

> You created resources in Azure but forgot where they are.
> **How would you identify and manage them?**

✅ Expected Answer:

* Azure Portal
* Resource Groups
* Tags
* Cost Management
